About this journal

I write because I can't not write.

This isn't a personal brand. There's no niche, no content calendar, no monetisation strategy. This is just me, 25 years old, trying to make sense of the particular madness of being alive right now.

started this because my notes app was getting embarrassing.

I write about the weeks that don't make it into the highlight reel. The 2am spirals, the quiet breakthroughs, the conversations that rewired something in me.

I believe that the most honest writing is the kind that embarrasses you a little. So here I am. Documenting it. Improperly, mostly. Welcome.
